Current Law Vulnerable

NYC Health Code §81.04

Requires purchasing solely from "approved sources," but mandates no farm-level or lot-code retention for raw leafy greens.

Federal Rule Delayed

FDA FSMA Section 204

Establishes Food Traceability List (FTL) with mandatory Critical Tracking Events (CTEs) and Key Data Elements (KDEs).

Proposed Policy Target Q1 2027

Article 81 NYC Amendment

NYC institutional kitchens must maintain electronic lot-tracking for high-risk produce starting January 2027.
